# Design System — U.P. Kirana Seva Samiti Balika Vidyalaya Website

## 1. Design concept
Traditional school identity meets a premium, contemporary editorial website. The experience should feel trustworthy, warm, spacious, elegant and school-specific—not like a generic template.

## 2. Brand cues
Use the supplied school logo and campus photo as reference assets. Preserve the logo's proportions and colours; do not distort it.

Core palette:
- Deep purple: primary brand colour
- Warm gold: accent
- Warm ivory/cream: main background
- White: cards/surfaces
- Dark charcoal: primary text
- Soft neutral grey: muted text and borders

Final hex values should be sampled from the supplied logo during implementation.

## 3. Typography
Use an elegant display typeface for major headings and a clean sans-serif for body/UI. Keep a clear hierarchy and readable line lengths.

## 4. Layout principles
- Spacious desktop container and generous section spacing
- Editorial storytelling for the school's legacy
- Avoid unnecessary card grids and crowded sections
- Homepage contains previews; detailed content lives on dedicated pages
- Design mobile layouts intentionally rather than simply shrinking desktop

## 5. Navigation
Primary links: Home, About, Academics, Campus, Events, Achievements, Alumni, More.
Secondary links can include Faculty, Gallery, Notices and School AI.
Parent Corner and Contact should be easy to find.

## 6. Homepage flow
1. Navigation
2. Hero with school identity and campus imagery
3. Key school statistics (mark estimates as approximate and verify them)
4. Short legacy/story preview
5. Featured event/calendar preview
6. Notice preview
7. Facilities preview
8. Achievements and student-life previews
9. Alumni preview
10. Parent Corner
11. Footer

## 7. Hero direction
Feature the school logo, full school name and motto “सेवा परमो धर्मः”. Use the campus photo as a visual anchor if image quality permits. Keep copy concise, contrast strong and calls to action clear.

## 8. Motion language
Use Motion / Motion Primitives selectively:
- Gentle hero text/image entrance
- Section reveal as content enters view
- Small card hover transitions
- Animated active state for tabs/filters
- Subtle number transitions where appropriate

Avoid continuous movement, excessive glow, heavy effects and animations that interfere with reading. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 9. Page-specific direction
- About: legacy and values storytelling
- Academics: clear class/stream structure
- Faculty: professional, consistent profiles (only verified details)
- Campus: large images with facility descriptions
- Events: readable calendar and event cards
- Notices: simple, accessible notice board
- Achievements: visual highlights
- Gallery: image-first layout with accessible captions
- Alumni: warm stories and achievements
- Parent Corner: utility-first and easy to scan
- School AI: restrained futuristic interface, clearly marked demo until real integration
- Contact: address, confirmed contact channels and map

## 10. Components
Define consistent styles for buttons, links, cards, section headings, badges, navigation, notice rows, event cards and footer.

## 11. Accessibility
Ensure readable contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image alt text, adequate touch targets and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status.

## 12. Responsive breakpoints
Use Tailwind responsive utilities and test mobile, tablet and desktop. Keep the most important navigation, notices, events and contact information easy to access on small screens.
