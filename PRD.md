# PRD — U.P. Kirana Seva Samiti Balika Vidyalaya Website

## 1. Product vision
Create a premium, responsive, multi-page website for U.P. Kirana Seva Samiti Balika Vidyalaya that presents the school's identity, academics, campus, activities, events, notices, achievements and alumni. The first release is a presentation-ready frontend prototype; student databases and ERP features are deliberately deferred until the school approves the concept.

## 2. School profile
- Name: U.P. Kirana Seva Samiti Balika Vidyalaya
- Type: All-girls senior secondary school
- Location: S-1, Site-2, Phase-III, Tatya Tope Nagar, Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh 208022
- Established: 2013
- Board: CBSE
- CBSE affiliation number: 2131948
- Management: U.P. Kirana Seva Samiti
- Principal: Mrs. Usha Sengar (verify before publication)
- Medium: English
- Classes: LKG to Class 12
- Senior secondary streams: PCM, PCB and Commerce
- Approximate student count previously supplied: 1,425–1,449 (verify before publication)
- Teacher count previously supplied: 65 (verify before publication)
- Approximate student–teacher ratio: 22:1 (verify before publication)
- Campus area previously supplied: 7,032 sq. m. (verify before publication)

## 3. Goals
1. Present the school as trustworthy, welcoming and contemporary.
2. Make essential information easy to find.
3. Provide a clear place for PTM information, notices, events and the academic calendar.
4. Showcase facilities, activities, achievements and student life.
5. Give alumni a dedicated presence.
6. Keep the frontend modular so a secure backend can be added later.

## 4. Target audiences
Prospective families, current parents, students, teachers, alumni, school management and general visitors.

## 5. Phase 1: prototype scope
- Home
- About / Our Story / Values / Principal
- Academics
- Faculty
- Campus & Facilities
- Events & Calendar
- Notices
- Achievements
- Gallery
- Alumni
- Parent Corner
- School AI demo
- Contact

The homepage previews each major area and links to its dedicated page; it should not contain every detail on one long page.

## 6. School-specific content
Use school diary material as the source for content planning, subject to verification:
- Academic calendar and holidays for 2026–27
- PTMs, tests, competitions, exhibitions and special assemblies
- Environmental initiatives and Earth Day activities
- Numeromania maths festival and class-wise activities
- School bell timings and seasonal timings
- Management committee and principal's message

Do not invent dates or present unverified information as official. Confirm the current calendar and details with school management before public launch.

## 7. Parent Corner in Phase 1
Show static/demo information for PTM dates and guidance, notices and holiday announcements, academic calendar, exam information, parent resources and reminders. Actual parent logins and automated email/SMS/push notifications are not part of Phase 1.

## 8. Explicitly out of scope for Phase 1
- Live student database
- Student promotion / class-change workflow
- Parent, student or teacher authentication
- Attendance, fees, marks or homework management
- Real notification delivery
- Full ERP
- Production AI connected to private school records

## 9. Phase 2 possibilities
After approval: secure database, academic-year enrolments, role-based accounts, attendance, results, homework, PTM slot booking, targeted notifications, admin dashboard and a school-data-grounded AI assistant.

## 10. UX and design principles
Premium but welcoming; school-specific; accessible; responsive; readable; simple for parents; subtle motion; no unnecessary complexity.

## 11. Success criteria
- Important information is discoverable in one or two interactions.
- Desktop and mobile layouts both work well.
- Navigation is consistent across pages.
- Real school details are verified before launch.
- Future backend integration can be added without rewriting all UI components.
